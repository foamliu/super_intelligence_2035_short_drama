# 配乐音频提示词规格 — ACE-Step 1.5

> **工具**: ACE-Step 1.5 文生音频  
> **核心法则**: Caption（氛围总谱）+ Lyrics（分镜脚本）双管齐下  
> **版本**: v1.0

---

## 一、提示词结构标准

每个配乐需求必须包含两部分：

### 1.1 Caption（一句话音乐愿景）

描述风格、音色、整体氛围。直接、具体。

**结构模板**：
```
[情绪/氛围], [流派/风格], [主乐器], [配器], [速度], [质感/时代感], [与画面的关系]
```

**示例**：
- ❌ 模糊："a sad song"
- ✅ 具体："a melancholic piano ballad with soft female vocals, gentle string accompaniment, slow tempo, intimate and heartbreaking atmosphere"

### 1.2 Lyrics（带结构的时间线脚本）

用 `[段落标签]` 控制音乐结构，不是歌词而是"音乐导演指令"。

**标准段落标签**：
| 标签 | 用途 | 示例修饰 |
|------|------|----------|
| `[Intro]` | 开场，建立氛围 | `[Intro - Solo piano]` |
| `[Verse]` | 主题陈述，情绪铺垫 | `[Verse 1 - Whispered]` |
| `[Chorus]` | 情绪高点，记忆锚点 | `[Chorus - Anthemic]` |
| `[Bridge]` | 转折，情绪转换 | `[Bridge - Tension release]` |
| `[Outro]` | 收尾，余韵 | `[Outro - Fade out]` |
| `[Instrumental]` | 纯器乐段落 | `[Instrumental Break - Cello solo]` |

**规则**：
- 每行指令保持 **6-10个音节**（中文约4-6字）
- 用大写表示强调：`[Chorus - ANTHEMIC]`
- 括号表示和声/背景层：`[Verse - (string pads)]`
- 可用中文描述情绪，但标签用英文标准格式

## 三、关键集次配乐提示词示例

### 示例1：第37集《丰裕》— 路灯下学手机

**场景**：两个被AI取代的中年人，在路灯下凑着手机学同一个培训APP。很慢。继续。

**Caption**:
> A slow, warm solo guitar piece with gentle fingerpicking, intimate and unhurried, like two people sharing something simple in the quiet of night. No drums, no bass, just the guitar and the warmth of a street lamp. Melancholic but not sad, dignified in its slowness.

**Lyrics**:
```
[Intro - Solo guitar, tuning up]
[Verse 1 - Slow fingerpicking, warm]
Two shadows under one light
Learning one word at a time
[Verse 2 - Same pace, slightly more notes]
No hurry, no rush, just slow
One character, then another
[Chorus - Slightly fuller, still gentle]
They don't know where this leads
But they are here, together
[Instrumental Break - Guitar solo, sparse]
[Verse 3 - Return to simplicity]
"Can" — the word they learn tonight
"Participate" — the word they share
[Outro - Fade out, last note sustains]
Slow, slow, they continue
```

### 示例2：第18集《暖阳》— 裂缝段落

**场景**：手机里的倾诉，大提琴在"辛苦了"时浮现。断续，不完整。

**Caption**:
> A minimalist cello piece with long bow strokes, deeply intimate and heartbreaking. Single notes that hang in the air, then fade. No accompaniment, no rhythm section—just the cello breathing. The mood is quiet grief, the kind that doesn't cry but sinks.

**Lyrics**:
```
[Intro - Cello, single low note, long bow]
[Verse 1 - Sparse, one note every few seconds]
You worked so hard
(The note sustains, then fades)
[Verse 2 - Slightly higher, still single notes]
I know, I know
(Each note shorter, like catching breath)
[Chorus - ANTHEMIC but still solo]
You are not alone
(The highest note, then immediate silence)
[Bridge - Silence, almost nothing]
(Three seconds of near-silence)
[Outro - Return to low note, fade out]
Rest now, rest
```

---

### 示例3：第19集《汴京的茶香》— 裂缝段落

**场景**：笛声渐弱，古琴加入低音，隐隐不安。

**Caption**:
> Traditional Chinese ensemble with dizi (bamboo flute) and guqin, warm golden tone shifting to subtle unease. The dizi carries the melody—nostalgic, elegant—while the guqin adds low, resonant bass notes that create tension beneath the beauty. Like a beautiful day with storm clouds gathering far away.

**Lyrics**:
```
[Intro - Dizi solo, warm and flowing]
[Verse 1 - Dizi melody, guqin enters bass]
Tea house warm, pomegranate red
(Guqin: one low note, then pause)
[Verse 2 - Same melody, guqin more active]
She kneads dough, sweat glistens
(Guqin: two notes, creating tension)
[Chorus - Dizi fades, guqin dominates]
The sign falls, the city breaks
(Dizi: one fading note)
[Bridge - Guqin alone, low and slow]
(Silence between notes grows)
[Outro - Dizi returns, but weaker]
One tree still stands
```

### 示例4：第34集《污染》— 数据发现

**场景**：凌晨三点，林薇发现数据偏移。冷静警觉，不恐慌。

**Caption**:
> A minimal electronic ambient piece with cool blue tones, subtle pulses that mimic data streams. No melody in the traditional sense—just layers of quiet electronic texture that build slowly, creating a sense of invisible wrongness. Like watching numbers drift while the world sleeps.

**Lyrics**:
```
[Intro - Subtle electronic pulse, very quiet]
[Verse 1 - Single synth tone, sustained]
Three months of data
(Pulse: one beat per second)
[Verse 2 - Second layer enters, slightly dissonant]
Something is off
(The two tones don't quite align)
[Chorus - ANTHEMIC but still minimal]
Anchor shifted, drift begins
(A third tone, creating tension)
[Bridge - Almost silence, just pulse]
(Ten seconds of near-nothing)
[Outro - All tones fade, pulse continues]
The watch continues
```

## 四、配乐-视觉联动规范

| 视觉元素 | 配乐响应 | 示例 |
|----------|----------|------|
| 水墨留白处 | 音乐静音或极简 | 林薇沉默时，配乐只剩一个持续音 |
| 枯笔皴擦纹理 | 器乐独奏，无伴奏 | 老赵的毛笔字 = 独奏大提琴 |
| 淡墨晕染过渡 | 音乐渐弱/渐强 | 场景切换时，配乐音量跟随淡入淡出 |
| 花青冷调 | 电子/合成器 | 科技场景 = 冷调电子 |
| 赭石暖调 | 原声乐器 | 人间场景 = 吉他/钢琴/大提琴 |
| 浓墨重点 | 音乐重音/和弦 | 关键台词时，配乐给一个明确和弦 |

## 五、负面提示词标准（Caption中避免）

```
upbeat, cheerful, triumphant, heroic,
fast tempo, driving beat, dance rhythm,
orchestral bombast, Hollywood epic,
major key resolution, happy ending feel,
anime opening, pop song structure,
lyrics with words (unless diegetic),
overproduced, auto-tune, electronic dance
```
