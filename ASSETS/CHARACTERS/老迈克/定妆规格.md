```
A 60-year-old Caucasian American man, retired steel mill worker,
square face with strong jaw and high cheekbones,
deep-set blue-grey eyes with crow's feet and bags underneath,
broad nose with slightly reddened tip from years of factory air,
rough weathered complexion with sun spots and age marks,
thinning grey-white hair combed back naturally,
broad-shouldered build with thick arms and slightly stooped posture,

neutral expression, not smiling but not harsh,
wearing a red and black flannel shirt,

shallow depth of field, realistic color grading, low saturation,
1280x720, 16:9 aspect ratio, photorealistic
```

### 关键视觉约束

- **手上有故事**：茧、烫伤疤、油污痕迹——这是三十年钢厂工人的"身份证"
- **不要"美国梦广告"里的老工人**：不是啤酒广告里慈祥的银发老人，是真实的、有分量的
- **鼻子微红**：车间里的化学物质和粉尘，对呼吸系统的影响写在脸上
- **灰度**：匹兹堡的色调偏灰偏冷（铁锈带的底色）
